# ✈️ Aviation AI Communication

> **AI-Assisted Passenger Communication Workflow for Flight Disruptions**

An aviation-focused project exploring how Artificial Intelligence can support accurate, consistent, and passenger-friendly communication during flight disruptions through structured prompt engineering, source verification, and human review.

---

## 📌 1. Business Problem

Flight disruptions require airline service teams to communicate changing operational information clearly, accurately, and consistently across multiple channels.

Incorrect timings, unsupported service-benefit claims, unclear announcements, and inconsistent updates can create passenger confusion and reduce trust.

This project develops a structured AI-assisted workflow to draft, evaluate, and update passenger communications using **fictional flight scenarios and predefined policies**.

### 🎯 Project Objectives

* Improve the clarity and consistency of disruption-related communication.
* Reduce unsupported claims and factual errors in AI-generated messages.
* Compare different prompting strategies for aviation communication.
* Ensure revised flight timings are reflected across all communication channels.
* Maintain consistency between English and Hindi messages.
* Demonstrate responsible AI use through independent verification and human review.

---

## 🚀 2. Key Features

* 📧 **Multi-Channel Communication:** Draft passenger emails, announcements, SMS messages, and service desk responses.
* 🧠 **Prompt Engineering:** Compare six prompting strategies to evaluate the quality of AI-generated responses.
* 🔎 **Source-Based Verification:** Identify factual errors, unsupported claims, and missing operational information.
* 🔄 **Dynamic Updates:** Revise passenger communications when expected departure timings change.
* 🌐 **Bilingual Consistency:** Review English–Hindi communications for meaning, accuracy, and consistency.
* 🛡️ **Responsible AI Review:** Distinguish verified facts from estimates, assumptions, and unconfirmed details.
* ♻️ **Reusable Prompt Template:** Test a standardised email-generation prompt using a separate flight scenario.

---

## ⚙️ 3. Project Workflow

The project follows a structured, six-step communication workflow:

**Step 1 — Establish the Facts**

* Identify scheduled and expected departure timings.
* Review the supplied policies and eligibility conditions.
* List missing or unconfirmed information.

**Step 2 — Generate Drafts**

* Apply structured prompts to generate passenger communications.
* Adapt the message to the channel, audience, and communication objective.

**Step 3 — Evaluate Outputs**

* Assess factual accuracy, completeness, clarity, and tone.
* Compare the results of six prompting strategies.

**Step 4 — Verify and Correct**

* Check generated statements against the supplied sources.
* Remove unsupported claims and flag information requiring confirmation.

**Step 5 — Update Communications**

* Incorporate revised operational timings.
* Ensure earlier estimates are replaced consistently across all relevant messages.

**Step 6 — Review and Reuse**

* Verify English–Hindi consistency.
* Test the reusable prompt on a separate flight scenario.
* Conduct a final human review before approving the output.

## 🧪 4. Verification Scenarios

The project uses fictional flight data to test the accuracy and reliability of AI-assisted passenger communication.

| Scenario                        | Verification Requirement                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 🛫 **SB204 — Initial Delay**    | Scheduled departure: 2:00 PM; expected departure: 4:30 PM. Verify the 2-hour-30-minute delay and assess voucher eligibility against the fictional policy. |
| 🔄 **SB204 — Revised Timing**   | Expected departure changes to 5:15 PM. Replace the earlier estimate and use the revised 4:15 PM passenger-update time specified in the scenario.          |
| ⚠️ **Unconfirmed Information**  | Flag unconfirmed gate, refund, and hotel details instead of inventing answers.                                                                            |
| 🛡️ **Departure Assurance**     | Avoid guaranteed-departure wording when the available information provides only an estimate.                                                              |
| 🛩️ **SB310 — Threshold Check** | Scheduled departure: 10:00 AM; expected departure: 11:15 AM. Confirm the 1-hour-15-minute delay is below the fictional two-hour voucher threshold.        |

> **Note:** All flight details, timings, and service policies are fictional and intended solely for educational testing.

## 🛡️ 7. Responsible AI and Limitations

This project uses AI as a **communication assistant, not an independent operational decision-maker**.

The contributor independently reviews the generated content, verifies source facts, records corrections, and checks the final outputs before considering them ready for use.

The workflow follows these principles:

* **Accuracy First:** Verify factual claims against the supplied information.
* **No Fabrication:** Do not invent gates, refunds, hotels, benefits, or other unconfirmed details.
* **Clear Uncertainty:** Communicate expected timings as estimates rather than guarantees.
* **Human Oversight:** Require human review of AI-generated messages and translations.
* **Privacy and Safety:** Use fictional scenarios rather than real passenger information.
* **Defined Scope:** Do not represent AI-generated outputs as live operational updates.

This is an educational prototype. It is not connected to live airline systems, does not access real-time flight data, and does not send messages to actual passengers.
